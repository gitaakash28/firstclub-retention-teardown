Make it right in one tap

FirstClub already has an AI assistant for support. When an item arrives bad today, the customer has to find it and explain what went wrong. This concept puts one button on the order and hands the assistant everything it needs, so most fixes take two taps and no conversation.

Today, in the real app

Screenshots from a fresh install on Android, 26 Oct 2026. Personal details are hidden.

Signing up

The default path asks for your phone number, age and food preferences before showing whether FirstClub delivers to you. Each screen can be skipped, but 24 reviewers say they found out only after entering their details, and 25 more are from cities FirstClub doesn't serve at all, which the store listing never mentions.

Getting help

Help is FAQs plus an AI assistant, and asking for a refund starts a back-and-forth. FirstClub's replies to Play Store complaints also point 72% of people to email. I had no past orders, so I couldn't test whether a delivered order has its own report-an-issue path. That's the first thing I'd confirm.
