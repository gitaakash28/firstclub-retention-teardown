What negative reviews are about

This page tracks the problem described in the FirstClub retention teardown, which is a fixed snapshot of reviews through 24 Oct 2026. Here, the job pulls new Play Store reviews every Monday, tags them with the same taxonomy using an LLM, and rebuilds this page (workflow). Written reviews skew critical; the trend matters more than the level. Verbatim reviews aren't reposted here because the page publishes without a human check. Reviews up to 24 Oct 2026 were tagged in one pass for the case study and later ones by the weekly job, so small theme shifts around that date may be tagging noise.
